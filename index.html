Technology with people in mind

My research focuses on trustworthy AI, mobile computing, and cybersecurity. I use AI and machine learning with ubiquitous modalities—including WiFi signals, cameras, and acoustics—to develop applications that protect sensitive personal data and secure AI systems.

I collaborate across transportation engineering, sociology, health, agriculture, and data science. I lead the X+ Intelligence Lab and am affiliated with the Cyber Defense and AI Lab.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from North Carolina State University in 2023, working with Prof. Muhammad Shahzad in the Wolfpack Interactive, Sensing, and Networking Lab and with Prof. Anupam Das in the Wolfpack Security and Privacy Research Lab. From 2023 to 2025, I was an assistant professor in Computer Science at UNC Pembroke.
